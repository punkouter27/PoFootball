Mobile portrait UI: before and after

SCN_MENU and SCN_GAME, scripted players, 2026-10-22 20:32:38. Captured by Tools → PoFootball → UI → Capture Screens (5 ratios) at 1080 wide, five portrait ratios. Boxes on the "after" shots are drawn from the elements' own bounds, recorded by the tool, not placed by hand. The final whistle is published by the tool and time frozen, so the field under the result is mid-game.

Tests: EditMode 369/369, PlayMode 35/35 (new: aspect sweep, notch, state flow ×6, console health, scene integrity ×3).

Live game: compact score row

  • Tag and timeouts now sit beside the score instead of above and below it: the row is 118 units, was 147, and the ribbon, banner band and hit meter all moved up 29 units with it.
  • Systems_HudLayout keeps every slot derived from the one above, so this was one constant plus the team block; the HUD warning measured the real height (118).

DEBUG sheet: clear of the transport, with a PHYSICS switch

  • The sheet used to dock on the footer and cover PAUSE and the speed chip. In a game it now ends above them (Systems_HudLayout.CARD_DOCK_BOTTOM).
  • PHYSICS shares the heading row, so it costs the sheet no height. TAB or a three-finger tap also opens the sheet.

PHYSICS on (new)

  • Each body: its real CircleCollider2D ring (thicker on the carrier), a velocity arrow a quarter of a second long, a facing tick and a red fatigue arc. The ball gets an orange velocity arrow while it moves.
  • Debug builds, game scenes only; nothing is built until the switch is first turned on.

Result: one sheet, three tabs

  • BOX SCORE, SCORING and HIGHLIGHTS are tabs of the result sheet. The WATCH HIGHLIGHTS chip that floated on another document between the sheet and the footer is gone, and the sheet no longer has to be lifted to clear it.
  • The call-sheet card no longer stays over the result after the final whistle.
  • "100% PASS" on the call sheet overflowed its 92-unit label at 21:9; the label is 116 now.

SCORING tab (new)

  • Points by quarter and every scoring play, with tries folded into the touchdown line ("TD PASS +68 yd · XP"). Pinned over the box score, so switching tabs never moves REMATCH; more than seven scores shows the latest and "+N earlier".

Highlights, opened from the tab

  • RESULT, Android Back, or the last highlight ending brings the result sheet back.

Settings sheet

  • Unchanged on screen. Its tabs now come from Systems_UiTheme.Tab / PaintTab, shared with the result sheet. Android Back closes it.

Menu

  • Unchanged on screen. Android Back with nothing open arms EXIT? on the MENU chip; a second press closes the app.

Fit audit, every screen × every ratio

Findings = text cut off + single lines overflowing + scrolling + controls out of thumb reach (top 40%). "Before" had no reach check, so its column counts the first three.
