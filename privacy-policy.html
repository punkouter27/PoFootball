Punkouter Software · Privacy policy

PoFootball

AI football, learned not scripted

Application ID com.punkoutersoftware.pofootball
Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

PoFootball does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It runs on your device. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. The one network request it makes is a weather lookup for a fixed stadium city, described below, which you can switch off.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information — no name, email, phone number or address.
  • It does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs or location.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.
  • It shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases.
  • It has no chat, no user accounts, no leaderboards and no way to share anything with other players.
  • It sends nothing to Punkouter Software, and nothing about you to any third party.

Live weather

Each game is played in one of twelve fixed stadium cities, chosen by the game itself. When Live weather is on (Settings > Broadcast), the app asks Open-Meteo for that city's current weather when a game starts, so the rain or snow on screen matches the real sky there. The request contains only the city's published latitude and longitude. As with any web request, Open-Meteo's servers see your device's IP address; nothing else about you or your device is sent, the app keeps nothing from the request but the weather it returns, and no account or key is involved. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy apply to that request.

Switch Live weather off and the app makes no network request at all; the weather is then made up by the game from its own random seed.

Commentary voice

If you switch on the commentary Voice, the play-by-play is read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app hands it the commentary text and nothing else; what your speech engine does with text is governed by its provider's policy.

What stays on your device

PoFootball may save ordinary game state — progress, settings, results — in the app's own private storage on your device. It never leaves the device, nobody else can read it, and uninstalling the app deletes it. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf because we never receive it in the first place.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission, used only for the live weather lookup above. It also requests VIBRATE, so the phone can buzz on a big hit (Settings > Broadcast > Haptics). It asks for no location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone permission. If a future version needs the network for anything else, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Children

PoFootball is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no data it does not knowingly collect anything from children of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Google Play listing as well.
